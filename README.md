## Introduction

PHERSU is the second chapter of the planned triptych *Exercitia Spiritualia Animae*. The project explores the subconscious (dreams), the conscious (persona), and the unconscious (shadows) in three freestanding chapters.

𐌘𐌄𐌓𐌔𐌖 [pher-su:] is the Etruscan root of the word “persona”. This world centers on self-representation, avatars, and online cultures. It is an experiment in navigating the web, and in the making and masking of the self in the digital realm.

PHERSU took its form as a labyrinth-like website during the art residency EXPANDED SPACES, organised by AADK Spain in the village of Nea Livera, in the Peloponnese, Greece, in December 2024. The residency was funded by the Culture Moves Europe mobility scheme.

The work is equal parts a travelogue, a game, an exercise in digital storytelling, and e-poetry. It is a collage, a synthesis of things read, seen, and found. Most of all, it is an experiment in self-projection into cyberspace, and in the interactivity between the inner and the outer, the flesh and the screen.

## Entering

Once inside [phersu.net](https://phersu.net), the homepage evokes a stratified landscape: sky, forest, water. From there, visible and invisible paths branch.

Three of those paths:

**Laconian Avatars.** The avatar, both as an embodied deity and as a digital alter ego.

**Expanded Hours.** When space is stretched, time is too. This part of the website attends to how the persona, the outer self, changes over time.

**Orpheus.** In the myth of Orpheus and Eurydice, it is the gaze that undoes them.

## Navigation

The site is a playful and serious bridge between the physical and the digital, rooted in the idea of expanding space. Space returns throughout: as a tool for orientation, as a memory of where one has been, or as a mirage of territories not yet known.

It is a collection of HTML experiences, interconnected by hyperlinks that are more or less visible. The visitor makes their own way by scrolling, typing, clicking, hovering, and staying.

## Some of the pages

**[avatar_star_poem](https://phersu.net/avatar_star_poem/)**  
An interactive video poem, floating in space.

<!-- ![avatar star poem](images/avatar-star-poem.png) -->

**[ocean_drop](https://phersu.net/ocean_drop/)**  
The internet as a worldwide junction where many languages meet, often in English. This page thinks about syntax, and about translating thoughts.

<!-- ![ocean drop](images/ocean-drop.png) -->

**[kitchen](https://phersu.net/kitchen/)**  
A sonic meditation and a mundane manifestation, contemplating how to become the best self through a warped 3D avatar.

<!-- ![kitchen](images/kitchen.png) -->

**[a_map](https://phersu.net/a_map)**  
A 3D map with a floating weather system formed from fragments of climate, identity, and human inquiries.

<!-- ![a map](images/a-map.png) -->

**[firstnames](https://phersu.net/firstnames)**  
Based on *Prénoms*, an HTML page from 2012 by Yann Van De Cruysen. Each picture is a screenshot of Google Image results taken in 2025 for a given first name. Guess the names.

<!-- ![firstnames](images/firstnames.png) -->

**[alt_rapture](https://phersu.net/alt_rapture/)**  
Described but not seen. A feed without images, where every picture has vanished and only its metadata remains.

<!-- ![alt rapture](images/alt-rapture.png) -->

**[reflected_immediacy](https://phersu.net/reflected_immediacy/)**  
A sonic poem, a shifting play of the inner and the outer.

<!-- ![reflected immediacy](images/reflected-immediacy.png) -->

**[grassroot_groupminds](https://phersu.net/grassroot_groupminds/)**  
An eternal chatroom.

<!-- ![grassroot groupminds](images/grassroot-groupminds.png) -->

**[id_card](https://phersu.net/id_card/)**  
A play with the information one can derive from official ways of proving identity, through its sonic transcription.

<!-- ![id card](images/id-card.png) -->

**[imgsearch](https://phersu.net/imgsearch/index.html)**  
*Image Search* quietly gathers pictures of things held by hands, revealing how algorithmic vision confuses touch with possession.

### Expanded Hours

The clouds open up. This branch explores development over time, and stays close to the earth. It reads the Peloponnese as an emotional landscape, imagines it as a psyche, and explores its outermost textures.

<!-- ![Expanded Hours](images/expanded-hours.png) -->

### Laconian Avatars

The landscape is its own complex character. Laconia opens through the windows of an imaginative desktop and its eco-operating system.

<!-- ![Laconian Avatars](images/laconian-avatars.png) -->

### Orpheus

In the myth of Orpheus, the gaze has tremendous pleasures and consequences. The poet enters the underworld to find Eurydice and bring her back to life. She is released on the condition that he does not look at her until they are above the ground. Driven by doubt and longing, he turns his head, sees her for a second, and sends her back.

## Showing the work

A minimal computer setup, focused on screen, keyboard, and mouse.

- A small desktop computer, installed at the foot of the table
- A short set of connections, with internet access by RJ-45 ethernet marked `[INTERNET]`
- A 19-inch 4:3 screen, flat or cathodic, at 1600×1200 or 1920×1440. Otherwise a standard 16:9 display at 1280p or 1920p
- A white mouse and a white keyboard
- Headphones
- A desk, and a chair that can be adapted so people of different sizes can sit with the piece

Reference for the computers: [installation notes on phersu.net](https://phersu.net/_docs/__Art_References_/!_installation_computers/)

This describes a single station. The installation can be multiplied, up to three computers.
